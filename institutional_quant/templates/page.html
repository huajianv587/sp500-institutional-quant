Daily monitor · weekly adjustment · monthly deployment
Daily selection: required inputs
  1. S&P 500 current universe export.
  2. Price Change 1D export.
  3. Price Change 1W export.
  4. Price Change 1M export.
  5. Alpaca paper positions, synchronized automatically.
Weekly adjustment: required inputs
  1. S&P 500 current universe export.
  2. Latest 1D / 1W / 1M return snapshot.
  3. Historical adjusted-close price history.
  4. Sector classification from the instrument export.
  5. Alpaca paper positions, synchronized automatically.
Monthly research: required inputs
  1. Historical S&P 500 membership export.
  2. Point-in-time financial statements.
  3. Point-in-time consensus estimates and revisions.
  4. Historical adjusted prices and corporate actions.
  5. Comparable companies, ownership and insider activity.
Full demonstration: required inputs
  1. S&P 500 universe and historical membership.
  2. Financial statements and estimates / revisions.
  3. Historical adjusted prices plus 1D / 1W / 1M returns.
  4. Peer, ownership and insider datasets where available.
  5. Alpaca paper account for preview only; approval remains manual.
